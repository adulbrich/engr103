This ladder lists the Python and Rust constructs allowed each week. It grows cumulatively: a construct introduced in an earlier week stays allowed in every later week. Anything not yet on the ladder must not appear in take-home work, so that everyone traces the same constructs and any AI-generated cleverness stays easy to spot. Students who arrive experienced may use anything already introduced, in either language, at any point.

## The weekly ladder

| Week | Newly allowed in Python | Newly allowed in Rust | Still fenced off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `print`; int, float, bool, str literals; binary literals; arithmetic operators (`+ - * /`) and precedence | `println!` with `{}` placeholders and the `fn main` wrapper; `i32`, `f64`, `bool`, `&str`; binary literals; arithmetic and precedence | variables, functions, conditionals, loops |
| 2 | integer division `//` and truncation; `%`; implicit coercion; variables and assignment; `UPPER_CASE` constants | integer division and truncation; `%`; `let`, `let mut`, shadowing; `const` | functions, conditionals, loops |
| 3 | `def` functions with parameters and return | `fn` functions with typed params and return; calling functions | conditionals, loops |
| 4 | local and function scope; comparisons; `and`, `or`, `not`; `if`/`else` | scope, nested `{ }` blocks, the call stack; comparisons; `&&`, `\|\|`, `!`; `if`/`else` | `elif`/`else if` chains, loops, collections |
| 5 | `elif` chains; `input`; `try`/`except`; `int()`/`float()` parsing; comparing floats with a tolerance | `else if` chains; `match`; `read_line`; `.parse()`; `Result`; comparing floats with a tolerance | loops, collections |
| 6 | `assert`; test functions | `assert!`; test functions | loops, collections |
| 7 | `while`; `for` over `range`; `break`/`continue`; nested loops | `while`; `for` over ranges; `break`/`continue`; nested loops | collections |
| 8 | strings (indexing, slicing, searching, building); characters; `list`; indexing; `len`; iteration; `append` | `String`/`&str` method subset; `char`; `Vec`; indexing; `.len()`; iteration; `push` | the memory model, references, dictionaries |
| 9 | aliasing (two names, one list); passing lists to functions | `&`/`&mut` references; moves; borrowing-lite; passing `Vec` to functions | dictionaries and maps |
| 10 | `dict` (advanced extra) | `HashMap` (advanced extra) | nothing new required |

## Fenced off for the whole course

These constructs never appear in course examples or take-home work, even after the week their topic is reached.

- **Python:** comprehensions, lambdas, and f-string format specs beyond the basics.
- **Rust:** traits, lifetimes, and generics; and any borrowing before week 9.
