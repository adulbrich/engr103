This is the weekly map of the course: eighteen lectures across ten weeks, two
per week (Monday and Friday) except week 3, which loses its Monday to the
holiday, and week 6, whose Friday slot holds the midterm. Each week lists its
lectures, the assignment due that week, and the recitation. Lecture names
link to the notes, which are read before class; the right column names the topic
or problem family.

The first half, through the midterm, teaches the foundations, functions, scope,
conditionals, errors, and testing without iteration; loops and data come after
the midterm. **All learning outcomes are met by the end of week 9;** the two
week-10 lectures are advanced extras, not required for the outcomes.

The weekly rhythm is a Monday lecture, then the recitation midweek (Tuesday to
Thursday, with that week's assignment due the Monday night before it), then a
Friday lecture. Week 1 is the one exception: there is no earlier Monday to hold a
deadline, so the ungraded recitation comes first and Assignment 1 is due at the
end of the week. Each recitation assesses the **previous** week's two lectures,
so every graded topic has had a full weekend and one dedicated assignment before
it is tested; the current week's Monday lecture is assessed the following week.
That one-week lag is why a recitation's topic always trails its lecture by a
week.
